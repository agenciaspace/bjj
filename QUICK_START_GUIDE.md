# Quick Start Guide - BJJ Academy App

A comprehensive guide for students and professors to get started with the BJJ Academy App.

## 📱 Getting Started

### Step 1: Access the App
1. Open the app in your browser
2. Click "Sign In" to create an account
3. Use Google OAuth for quick registration

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile

**For Students:**
1. **Upload a Profile Photo**
   - Click the camera icon on your avatar
   - Choose a clear, front-facing photo
   - The photo will automatically be used for face recognition

2. **Set Your Belt Information**
   - Select your current belt (White → Black)
   - Add your current degrees (0-4)
   - This helps track your progress

3. **Choose Your Role**
   - Select "Student" if you're a regular member
   - Select "Professor" if you own or teach at an academy

### Step 3: Join an Academy

**As a Student:**
1. Navigate to your Profile page
2. Find the "Join Academy" section
3. Enter the **6-character join code** provided by your professor
4. Click "Join Academy"
5. Wait for approval from the academy owner

**As a Professor/Owner:**
1. On your Profile page, find "Academy Management"
2. Click "Create Academy" if you don't have one
3. Enter your academy name
4. Share the generated **join code** with your students

## 🎓 For Students

### Logging Training Sessions
1. Go to the **Home** page
2. Fill in the training form:
   - Duration (in minutes)
   - Technique practiced
   - Notes about the session
   - Training type (Gi, No-Gi, etc.)
3. Click "Add Training"
4. View your recent activity on the home page

### Checking In at Academy
1. Go to the **Check-In** page
2. Either:
   - **Manual**: Click "Check In Now" to record your presence
   - **Face Recognition**: Use camera for automatic check-in (requires enrollment)
3. Your check-in is recorded and synced

### Viewing Your Progress
1. Visit your **Profile** page
2. See statistics:
   - Total training sessions
   - Hours trained
   - Current streak
   - Check-in count
3. View unlocked achievements
4. Track belt progression

### Using the Training Timer
1. Go to the **Timer** page
2. Set the duration for rolling rounds
3. Start the timer for sparring sessions
4. Timer works even offline

### Face Recognition Setup
1. On your **Profile** page, find "Facial Recognition Settings"
2. Click "Enable Face Recognition"
3. Allow camera access when prompted
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to enroll your face
5. After enrollment, you can check in with face recognition

**Tips:**
- Use good lighting
- Keep your face centered in the frame
- Remove sunglasses/hats
- Ensure camera is at eye level

## 👨‍🏫 For Professors/Owners

### Creating Your Academy
1. On your **Profile** page, go to "Academy Management"
2. Click "Create Academy"
3. Enter your academy name
4. Your unique **6-character join code** is generated automatically
5. Share this code with students who want to join

### Managing Join Requests
1. On your **Profile** page, view "Pending Requests"
2. Review each student's profile
3. Click **Approve** to accept them
4. Click **Reject** to decline
5. Approved students become active members

### Promoting Students
1. View your active members list
2. Click "Promote" next to a student
3. Select:
   - New belt level
   - Number of degrees (0-4)
4. Confirm the promotion
5. Student's profile is updated immediately

### Using Class Check-In
1. Navigate to **Class Check-In** page (available to professors/owners)
2. Hold up your camera to the class
3. The system automatically:
   - Detects all faces in the frame
   - Identifies enrolled students
   - Records their attendance
4. View the attendance list after scanning

### Viewing Academy Statistics
1. Click on your academy name from the profile
2. See:
   - Total members
   - Members by belt level
   - Owner information
   - Join code

### Managing Members
- View all active members with their belt information
- See member photos
- Track member count
- Promote students as they progress

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Can't Join an Academy?
- Verify the join code is correct (6 characters, uppercase)
- Ensure the academy exists
- Contact your professor if the code doesn't work
- Check your internet connection

### Face Recognition Not Working?
- Ensure you've enrolled your face first
- Check camera permissions in browser settings
- Use good lighting
- Remove accessories that cover your face
- Try enrolling again if it fails

### Profile Photo Not Uploading?
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure photo is under 2MB
- Try a different image format (JPG, PNG)
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Data Not Syncing?
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Data syncs automatically when online
- Check Supabase status if issues persist

### Can't See Academy Information?
- Ensure you've been approved by the professor
- Check your member status (should be "active")
- Wait a few minutes and refresh the page

## 📱 Mobile App Installation

The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA):

### On iOS (iPhone/iPad):
1. Open the app in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
5. App icon will appear on your home screen

### On Android:
1. Open the app in Chrome
2. Tap the **Menu** button (three dots)
3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Confirm installation
5. App will be added to your app drawer

### Benefits of Installing:
- Works offline
- Faster loading
- Full-screen experience
- No browser bar
- Icon on home screen

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

### Face Recognition Data
- Face embeddings are encrypted
- Only you can delete your face data
- Data is stored securely in Supabase
- You can disable face recognition at any time

### Profile Information
- Your data is protected by Row Level Security
- Only authorized users can see your information
- You control what you share
- Google OAuth provides secure authentication
